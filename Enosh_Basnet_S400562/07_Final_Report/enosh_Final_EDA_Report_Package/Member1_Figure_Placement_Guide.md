## Practical placement rules for final group-document integration

1. Keep each original image together with its academic caption; do not distort its aspect ratio.
2. Cite the existing figure number in the body rather than renumbering the standalone PNG.
3. Retain a legible image width, particularly for the eight-panel Figure 5. If space is limited, place that figure on its own page rather than shrinking labels to unreadable size.
4. Keep the 103-match sensitivity display labelled **visual only**; the official dataset includes Match 13.
5. If the group report drops a figure for space, retain the corresponding quantitative finding in the prose/table and update all figure cross-references.
